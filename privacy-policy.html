Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This notice explains which personal data is used when you visit this website or get in touch, why it is used, and the choices and rights available to you.

Privacy at a glance

  • The website only tracks things like IP address/technical data or the data you submit. The website’s analytics are cookie-free, and no other personal data is tracked.
  • And that data is only used for processing your request, to contact you and improve the website.
  • Within my business, only I have access to the information. Service providers like WhatsApp or my E-mail provider have DPAs (data processing agreements) with me when they handle data on my behalf. These agreements require them to follow my instructions, keep your data confidential and take strict security measures.
  • I delete your data as soon as I can. For enquiry and booking data, this is usually when follow-up ends.
  • At any time, you can email me at info@milangrubor.com to ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data. Also of course to withdraw consent.
Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Igor Grubor, trading as Milan Grubor. The business address is stated in the Impressum. Correspondence concerning the processing of personal data may be addressed to info@milangrubor.com.

Website operation and usage statistics

Website hosting and network security providers process technical request data, including IP addresses, requested pages and request timestamps, to deliver and secure the website. Identifiers derived from IP addresses and email addresses submitted through forms are used to limit repeated requests and prevent misuse. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interests pursued are the reliable operation of the website and the protection of its forms against misuse. Where access or security logs are stored, they are retained for [confirmed retention period or criteria].

A selected language preference is used to display the website in that language. Unsubmitted application data are held in the open page while the applicant moves between steps. The data are transmitted upon submission of a booking or WhatsApp contact request.

Aggregate server-side traffic reports include request volumes by country. The hosting provider uses IP addresses to estimate the country from which requests originate. Selected website requests are also counted for statistical purposes. The counts cover loads of the home, application, FAQ, privacy policy and legal notice pages, calendar availability requests, valid booking attempts, confirmed bookings and successful WhatsApp contact requests. The statistics retained consist of weekly totals for each activity. Repeated requests may be counted separately. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is evaluating and improving the website and booking process.

Enquiries, applications and appointments

Names, email addresses, any salutation supplied and the contents of enquiries or applications are processed to respond to submissions and arrange the requested contact. Website form processing services handle submissions. Where email correspondence is sent, the providers used for sending or receiving it process the information required for that correspondence.

If a video appointment is booked, Nylas processes contact details, the selected appointment time and the time zone. The website checks calendar availability and submits bookings to Nylas from its server. Appointment details are transmitted to Apple iCloud Calendar. Zoom processes meeting details and transmits audio and video during the call. Plunk sends booking confirmations and reminders.

To prevent duplicate video appointments, the website keeps a hash derived from the booking email address, an appointment reference and booking timing information while an appointment or booking attempt is active. This record does not contain the email address itself. The booking calendar is checked before accepting another call. Expired records are removed during routine booking processing; cancellations are checked when another booking is requested.

If contact via WhatsApp is requested, the telephone number and submitted information are processed to respond to the request. WhatsApp and the business messaging provider handle the contact details and communications necessary to provide that channel.

The controller will conclude a data processing agreement (DPA) under Article 28 GDPR with any messaging provider acting on the controller’s behalf before the provider begins processing personal data. The agreement requires the provider to follow documented instructions, maintain confidentiality and implement appropriate security measures.

Processing necessary to take steps at the applicant’s request prior to entering into a coaching contract is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other enquiry processing and the use of optional correspondence details are based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is responding to correspondence and arranging the requested contact. The details marked as required are needed to respond through the selected channel or arrange the appointment. Other information may be provided voluntarily.

Newsletter

A confirmation email is sent to the address supplied at registration. Your subscription starts when you click on the confirmation link. The newsletter delivery provider processes your email address, subscription status and delivery information for the administration and distribution of the newsletter. Open and click tracking for newsletter engagement is disabled.

The legal basis is consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in each newsletter or by emailing me at info@milangrubor.com.

Retention

Enquiry and appointment records are deleted once the matter is closed and further correspondence or follow-up is unnecessary. If a coaching contract is concluded, any data required for coaching are retained in accordance with the privacy notice provided during onboarding.

Newsletter addresses are retained while registration is pending or the subscription is active. Unconfirmed registrations are deleted when the registration request is closed. Following withdrawal, the minimum information necessary to honour the unsubscribe request and document prior consent is retained for as long as required for those purposes.

Records are retained for longer where required by law under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend specific legal claims under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
