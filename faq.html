Frequently asked questions

Below is a contact form if these don't answer your question.

What does coaching include?

Everything you will need to achieve your fitness goals. This can include a nutrition plan with exact macros or even food choices if you'd like, a training plan with exercises, sets and form guidance, a supplement regimen, and additional advice on habits. These are a few examples of what we can work on together. We can also cover most other things that would help you reach your goals.

How much does coaching cost?

The current rate is €200 per month, with a three-month minimum commitment. Of course, you retain your right of withdrawal if applicable.
After three months, you can cancel at any time, effective from the start of the following month.

Who is coaching for?

Coaching is for people who want to build muscle, lose fat or bring more structure to their training. You can find more detail in Who I coach.

How do I apply?

Choose Apply, enter your contact details, then choose how you would like to get in touch.

Can I choose a call instead of WhatsApp?

Yes. The application lets you choose between arranging a call and requesting contact on WhatsApp.
