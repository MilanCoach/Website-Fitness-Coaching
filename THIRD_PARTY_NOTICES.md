# Third-party software

`assets/vendor/libphonenumber-max.js` bundles **libphonenumber-js 1.13.15**, used for international phone validation in the browser and Worker. Source: https://github.com/catamphetamine/libphonenumber-js. License: MIT, reproduced in `assets/vendor/libphonenumber-LICENSE.txt`.

The bundled library runs locally without contacting a phone-validation service. Development dependencies and their licenses are listed in `package-lock.json` and installed packages.
